# Design System: WP Pivot Research

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Distilled Bench Specimen"**

WP Pivot Research is a contemporary service manual for evidence-led Metro restoration. Warm paper, black ink, measured rules, and Lumia blue make the work feel documented and inspectable; Metro appears through typographic confidence, direct hierarchy, horizontal rhythm, and purposeful asymmetry rather than period-costume imitation.

The homepage distills that world into a two-column editorial split: the literal project title “Rebuilding the Windows Phone UI in Flutter.” beside one published Pivot study. The title names the work directly rather than acting as a thesis, campaign slogan, or tagline. Its supporting sentence states the project purpose in plain language. The study leads with a light, oversized headline, a short explanation, and one primary action; source details and limitations live in the linked study rather than being repeated on the homepage. A shallow research index follows. The shipping surface remains static HTML and CSS, with no generated raster used as content or decoration.

Internal pages extend the same Bench Specimen identity as readable evidence manuals. A shared title-and-intro mast establishes each page, then repeated label/body rails assign one concise subject to each section. Index pages use featured records, factual record rows, status labels, and explicit empty statements. The published Pivot study adds a fact strip, tables, downloadable evidence, and captioned media while keeping limitations next to the claims they qualify.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm paper surfaces with black ink and flat technical rules.
- Lumia blue used sparingly for links, focus, selection, and active navigation.
- Segoe-led typography that separates a dense literal project title from a light editorial study headline.
- One source-backed study feature with a short explanation and one action.
- Shared internal-page masts followed by ruled manual sections with stable label and evidence columns.
- Record inventories, study facts, and captioned media that keep sources and limitations visible.
- Published, available, pending, and planned states stated without visual or verbal inflation.
- A responsive title-to-study sequence followed by a shallow research index.

## Colors

The palette resembles ink and annotation on a warm service-manual sheet: neutral structure does most of the work, while blue, amber, and green communicate precise roles.

### Primary

- **Lumia Blue:** Focus outlines, selection, and the active navigation rule.
- **Deep Lumia Blue:** Links, research-index labels, and published or in-progress state text that needs quieter emphasis.

### Secondary

- **Planned Amber:** Pending limits and planned work; it signals incompleteness without turning it into an alarm.
- **Available Green:** The established available-status color on internal research tables.

### Neutral

- **Warm Paper:** The page ground and default component surface.
- **Deep Paper:** Hover feedback and inline-code surfaces.
- **Service Ink:** Primary text, strong borders, and dark code surfaces.
- **Muted Ink:** Supporting text and empty-status language.
- **Measured Rule:** Structural dividers between editorial zones, mast columns, and manual-section rails.
- **Light Rule:** Record rows, study facts, media captions, tables, and research-index separators.
- **White:** Selection text, skip-link text, and content on dark technical surfaces.

### Named Rules

**The Ink-and-Blue Rule.** Paper, black ink, and rules carry the page; Lumia blue is reserved for links, focus, selection, and active navigation.

**The Status Honesty Rule.** Amber means planned or pending work, and green means available work; never use an accent to imply verification that the source record does not support.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Segoe UI Variable, with Segoe UI and Selawik fallbacks

**Body Font:** Segoe UI Variable, with Segoe UI and Selawik fallbacks

**Label/Mono Font:** Segoe UI Variable for labels; Cascadia Code with Consolas fallback for code

**Character:** One Segoe-led family carries the editorial hierarchy through scale, weight, and tracking. The literal homepage title is dense and declarative, internal index titles are equally confident, and study titles and manual-section labels use light editorial weights. Compact labels identify evidence and status without competing with the reading text. Code switches to a familiar Microsoft-oriented monospace stack.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (600, desktop clamp 3.4rem–4.8rem, 0.94 line-height): The literal three-line project title. On mobile it uses a separate 3.1rem–4rem clamp (`clamp(3.1rem, 13vw, 4rem)`) so the title remains readable without becoming a campaign-style headline.
- **Headline** (350, responsive 3rem–5.2rem, 0.98 line-height): The published study title; its lighter weight distinguishes evidence presentation from the thesis.
- **Page Title** (600, responsive 4rem–6rem, 0.94 line-height): Internal index-page titles in the left side of the shared mast.
- **Study Page Title** (350, responsive 3.8rem–6rem, 0.94 line-height): The published study's title, lighter than index titles; both internal title roles use the same 3.6rem–5rem mobile override.
- **Manual Heading** (350, responsive 2rem–3.35rem, 1 line-height): Section subjects in the left label rail, normally constrained to 10 characters wide.
- **Title** (650, 1.15rem, 0.055em tracking): Uppercase service-manual section headers on internal pages.
- **Lead** (400, 1.25rem, 1.5 line-height): The homepage's concise project explanation, constrained to 36 characters.
- **Page Deck** (400, responsive 1.2rem–1.55rem, 1.45 line-height): One or two introductory paragraphs in the right side of an internal-page mast, constrained to 48 characters.
- **Summary** (400, 1.15rem, 1.55 line-height): The published study's concise framing paragraph, constrained to 46 characters.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.55 line-height): Explanatory copy, lists, and table content, normally constrained to 72 characters.
- **Label** (650, 0.78rem, 0.07em tracking, uppercase): Primary navigation and compact technical controls.
- **Action** (650, 0.82rem, 0.055em tracking, uppercase): The single rule-backed action beneath the study summary.
- **Code** (400, 0.9em): Inline code uses Deep Paper; code blocks invert to Service Ink with White text.

### Named Rules

**The Segoe-without-Redistribution Rule.** Reference Segoe UI Variable and Segoe UI when installed, fall back to the OFL-licensed Selawik package, and never bundle proprietary Segoe font files.

**The Two-Voices Rule.** Set the literal project title in a dense semibold display and the study headline in a light editorial weight; do not flatten both sides into the same typographic voice.

## Layout

The homepage uses a centered sheet up to 1520px wide with 16px outer gutters. Its first section is a two-column editorial split with a minimum height of 650px: the literal project title and description occupy the narrower 0.95 fraction, and the published study occupies the wider 1.35 fraction. A one-pixel vertical rule separates them. The study feature is vertically centered and keeps its summary to a readable 46-character measure. A four-column research index continues beneath the split.

Internal pages use the same centered 1520px shell. Their shared mast pairs a narrower title rail and wider intro rail at 0.72 / 1.28 with a 360px minimum height; the published study mast grows to 520px. Manual sections repeat a 0.38 / 1 label-to-body grid separated by a vertical rule. Featured records and ordinary record rows use three aligned columns for name, detail, and status. Study facts use four equal columns, while media evidence fills the body rail and keeps its caption directly beneath the image or video.

At 1050px and below, the homepage ratio tightens to 0.9 / 1.1, internal masts tighten to 0.58 / 1, and manual sections tighten to 0.3 / 1. At 760px and below, all paired structures stack with 12px outer gutters: the mast title becomes a minimum 290px block above its intro, each section label sits above its body, record rows collapse to one column, study facts become two columns, media captions stack, and tables scroll horizontally. Below 460px, the homepage index becomes one column.

**The Title-and-Evidence Rule.** Keep the literal project title beside one source-backed study on wide screens; on smaller screens, preserve the same title-then-study reading order.

**The Rules-Are-the-Grid Rule.** Use one-pixel dividers, open space, and alignment to establish grouping instead of card backgrounds or floating containers.

**The Mast-to-Rails Rule.** Open every internal page with one shared title-and-intro mast, then move through repeated label/body sections; keep each section's subject in the label rail and its evidence or explanation in the body rail.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is entirely flat. It uses no decorative shadows, gradients, glass effects, or stacked card elevation. Depth comes from warm tonal shifts, black and gray rules, typographic contrast, and the asymmetry between the compact title column and expansive study feature.

**The Flat-Page Rule.** Every surface rests on the same editorial sheet; hierarchy comes from typography, rules, and contrast, never simulated elevation.

## Shapes

The form language is rectilinear and open. Navigation, the study feature, evidence rows, tables, links, and index cells are shaped by straight rules rather than rounded containers. The system has no reusable radius scale.

**The Open-Edge Rule.** Keep content unboxed where possible; use a single separating rule or tonal hover shift instead of pills, framed panels, or decorative containers.

## Components

### Navigation

- **Style:** A 66px-high header with the brand on the left and uppercase technical links on the right.
- **Default:** Service Ink text with no decorative container.
- **Hover / Active:** A 3px Lumia Blue rule appears on the bottom edge; the current page also carries `aria-current`.
- **Mobile:** The header stacks, and the link row scrolls horizontally at a 48px minimum height.

### Project Title

- **Style:** The literal title “Rebuilding the Windows Phone UI in Flutter.” set across three lines, with a direct project-purpose sentence beneath it.
- **Typography:** Dense semibold display type contrasts with the lighter study headline across the dividing rule; it is a project title, not a thesis, slogan, or tagline.
- **Responsive:** The title remains in the first reading position, receives a minimum height of 590px when stacked, and uses the mobile display override (`clamp(3.1rem, 13vw, 4rem)`).

### Published Study Feature

- **Style:** A vertically centered editorial feature with a light oversized headline, concise summary, and one primary action.
- **Spacing:** 72px vertical padding on wide screens and responsive horizontal padding up to 96px; mobile uses 52px above and 64px below.
- **Content:** Name one published study and report differences plainly. Keep source details, counts, versions, and unresolved limits in the linked study instead of repeating them here.

### Page Mast

- **Style:** A shared internal-page header with a title rail on the left and one concise introductory deck on the right, divided by a Measured Rule.
- **Index Variant:** Semibold page titles use the Page Title role inside a 360px-minimum mast.
- **Study Variant:** The mast grows to 520px, uses the light Study Page Title role, and may add a four-item study-facts strip beneath the deck.
- **Responsive:** Below 760px, the title becomes a minimum 290px block above the intro; both title variants use the same mobile clamp.

### Manual Sections

- **Style:** Repeated label/body evidence rails separated vertically on wide screens and stacked on mobile.
- **Label Rail:** A light Manual Heading names one subject; avoid summaries or status prose in this rail.
- **Body Rail:** Owns the explanation, records, tables, evidence media, downloads, and qualifications for that subject.
- **Responsive:** Below 760px, the label moves above the body and the vertical divider disappears.

### Featured Records and Record Rows

- **Featured Record:** Strong top and bottom rules, a large Deep Lumia Blue name, concise detail, and a trailing status; hover uses Deep Paper without changing text color.
- **Record Row:** Name or link, muted metadata, and optional status align in three columns with Light Rule separators.
- **Responsive:** Both collapse to one column with an 8px gap on mobile; source and state remain adjacent to the record they describe.

### Study Facts

- **Style:** Four compact definition items beneath the study deck, each with an uppercase Muted Ink term and medium-weight value.
- **Content:** Reference, OS build, evidence count, and hardware state are allowed only when backed by the study sources.
- **Responsive:** Four columns on wide screens and two columns with Light Rule separators on mobile.

### Media Evidence

- **Style:** Full-width image or video in the section body rail with a restrained caption directly below.
- **Caption:** The evidence name is Service Ink and the download link or source note is Muted Ink; the row ends on a Light Rule.
- **Responsive:** Caption contents stack with a 4px gap on mobile. Never detach a qualification from the media it describes.

### Primary Research Link

- **Style:** An uppercase text action distributed across a thin bottom rule, with a light arrow at the trailing edge.
- **Shape:** Rectilinear and open; no fill or rounded container.
- **Hover / Focus:** The arrow moves 6px to the right over 180ms; the global 2px Lumia Blue focus outline remains visible.

### Status Labels

- **Style:** Compact uppercase text without a pill or background.
- **State:** Deep Lumia Blue is the default published or in-progress emphasis, Planned Amber marks planned work, Available Green marks availability, and Muted Ink marks empty or unpublished work.
- **Content:** Use only repository-supported states and place the source beside any published result.

### Research Index Links

- **Style:** Flat, border-separated content links with a Deep Lumia Blue uppercase title and short Service Ink description.
- **Hover / Focus:** Hover shifts the cell from Warm Paper to Deep Paper; keyboard focus uses the global Lumia Blue outline.

### Code Surfaces

- **Inline:** Deep Paper behind compact monospace text with narrow inline padding.
- **Block:** Service Ink with White text and 18px padding; horizontal overflow remains available for long lines.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** preserve the warm-paper, black-ink, Lumia-blue palette and let flat rules organize the page.
- **Do** keep the homepage focused on one literal project title, one published study, and one primary action.
- **Do** keep detailed counts, sources, measurements, comparisons, downloads, and limitations in the linked study rather than duplicating them on the homepage.
- **Do** give each internal section one concise label and keep its evidence, explanatory copy, and status in the corresponding body rail.
- **Do** use the Segoe UI Variable, Segoe UI, Selawik, system-ui stack without shipping proprietary Segoe font files.
- **Do** trace every study value and status to repository evidence, including the unresolved Lumia validation limit.
- **Do** preserve visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior, and mobile reading order.

### Don't:

- **Don't** turn the system into a period recreation or use Metro as decorative nostalgia.
- **Don't** add decorative cards, rounded pills, gradients, glass effects, or shadows.
- **Don't** add illustrative stand-ins that could be mistaken for captured or measured evidence.
- **Don't** use a manual-section label rail for long copy, evidence values, or multiple competing subjects.
- **Don't** present emulator evidence as Lumia hardware validation or imply a finding before its source exists.
- **Don't** ship generated raster composition, generated copy, invented measurements, sample dates, or demonstration data as research content.
